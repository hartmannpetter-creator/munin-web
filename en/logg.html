Logbook

What Munin has seen.

An open log of the development: reports from real drives, milestones in the system, and findings along the way. Updated as the work progresses.

September 2026 · Milestone · Freelander 2 · TD4

The on-board AI chip has started learning

After months of data collection, the car has enough driving data for every signal group to be trained. Each group gets its own model on the on-board AI chip, trained solely on this car's healthy state. The first ones are done, and the rest will follow. From there, Munin can see when a pattern starts to drift, before it becomes a fault.

September 2026 · Report · Freelander 2 · TD4

First monthly report, no human involved

All three report types are now working, and the first monthly report was written and sent entirely on its own. From the moment the schedule triggered until the PDF landed in the inbox, nobody touched it. The report arrived a few drives after the thermostat was replaced, and closes the loop from June: Munin found the leaking thermostat, the replacement was logged in the service record, and Munin now confirms that the new one holds the temperature steady where it should. Finding, repair and confirmation in one system.

June 2026 · Image

The journey to the next generation has begun

Munin couldn't keep up with its tasks on a Raspberry Pi 5. So we're biting the bullet and migrating to new hardware: a platform with a CPU and a GPU built for AI on the same device.

June 2026 · Milestone

Munin learns its own healthy state

The system got a fixed reference point for how the car looks when it is healthy, so a gradual change can be measured against something stable instead of slipping by unnoticed. Munin freezes this point on its own once the car has shown what it needs to, and can reset it when a part is actually replaced.

June 2026 · Milestone

Service reminders tied to what the car actually needs

A dedicated maintenance track with intervals per car and a service log the system itself helps keep in order. When something is replaced, this tells Munin to learn the car's new healthy state from scratch.

June 2026 · Report · Freelander 2 · TD4

Thermostat fault found before it set a fault code

From three months of driving data, several independent signs pointed to the same failure: the engine did not reach the correct temperature under load, and dropped unusually fast when the foot came off the throttle. A leaking thermostat, caught long before it would have triggered a warning light. Munin's first real field diagnosis.

June 2026 · Report

An important gap in the system found and closed

A review revealed that the anomaly detection compared the car against itself in the moment, not against a healthy past. A car with a fault from day one would then learn the fault as normal. The finding triggered the entire work on the fixed reference point.

June 2026 · Milestone · Freelander 2 · TD4

Assessing a used car before you buy it

Added a third report type, used-car assessment: Munin combines the car's own measurements with known weaknesses for that exact model and year, gathered from outside sources. Instead of just reading the car in front of you, it knows what to look for on that particular type of car.
